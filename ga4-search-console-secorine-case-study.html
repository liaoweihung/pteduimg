中文報告

背景與問題

2026 年 11 月 12 日至 18 日，藥局衛教助手的「息咳寧糖漿」頁面在 GA4「網頁和畫面」報表以 98 次瀏覽居首，占報表中 587 次瀏覽的 16.7%，有 48 位活躍使用者。最初想知道讀者如何抵達這一頁，以及接著去了哪裡。進一步查入口時，GA4 顯示 48 個工作階段以息咳寧為入口並歸因給 google / organic，Search Console 的網頁文字搜尋則記錄 16 次點擊。本報告記錄查核過程、全站對照，以及現有報表能支持的結論。 息咳寧糖漿頁面

查核方式

  1. 固定日期與網站範圍。 兩套報表都選 2026/11/12–11/18。GA4 資源使用台灣時間（UTC+8），Search Console 每日資料使用太平洋時間，本期間為 PST（UTC−8）。相同日期標籤的邊界相差 16 小時。全站比較限定在 liaoweihung.github.io 的 /pteduimg/ 路徑，同網域的其他工具另計。 Google 的報表差異說明
  2. 用 GA4 查瀏覽、站內路徑和入口。 在「網頁和畫面」讀取瀏覽量，在「路徑探索」以頁面標題分別設定起點與終點。在「任意形式」篩選 到達網頁 完全符合 /pteduimg/cards/ped_cold_secorine.html，以及 工作階段來源／媒介 完全符合 google / organic。全站改用入口路徑規則運算式 ^/pteduimg/.*$，並限定正式主機。另以 事件名稱 = session_start 分組查看工作階段開始時的網頁參照網址。 GA4 路徑探索 · GA4 工作階段與歸因
  3. 用 Search Console 查點擊、搜尋類型與標準網址。 在「搜尋結果」成效報表，單頁用完整網址完全比對，全站用 https://liaoweihung.github.io/pteduimg/ 作網址前綴。分別查網頁文字、圖片、網頁多模態、影片與新聞搜尋。查看逐日、裝置、國家分組，另用網址審查工具核對 Google 選定的標準網址。 Search Console 搜尋成效與搜尋類型
  4. 用站內其他頁作對照，再抽查兩天。 分別從全站工作階段數與各搜尋類型點擊數扣除息咳寧，得出其他頁合計。針對 11/12、11/14 的 7 個工作階段，查看地區、城市、作業系統、瀏覽器、參與時間與「新／舊使用者」，並用「首次造訪」指標 (first_visit) 交叉核對。 GA4 使用者指標

結論

息咳寧的 GA4 工作階段與 GSC 點擊落差比其他頁大，且 GA4 互動率也較高。 息咳寧為 48 個工作階段對 20 次點擊（2.40 倍），其他頁為 24 對 17（1.41 倍）。互動率分別為 71% 和 33%。這些比值用於比較報表落差，沒有通用的正常門檻。互動率反映工作階段是否符合 GA4 條件，無法證明讀者身分、閱讀意圖或衛教效果。 GA4 互動率定義

差距的來源尚未確認。現有報表無法逐筆配對點擊與工作階段、量化 Discover，也不足以排除追蹤設定問題或確認 Search Console 漏計。兩天的 7 次 first_visit 只確認 GA4 辨識為新訪，並未逐筆核對這 7 次的引薦網址或確認有 7 位不同的真人。平常用 Search Console 看曝光、點擊、查詢字詞和排名，用 GA4 看進站後的參與和站內瀏覽。這次查核只使用既有報表，公開紀錄採彙總數字。 Google 對兩份報表的使用建議 · GA4 報表識別方式

Why GA4 sessions and Search Console clicks differ

Background and question

From November 12 to 18, 2026, the Secorine Syrup page on the Pharmacy Education Assistant ranked first in the GA4 Pages and screens report. It received 98 views, or 16.7% of the 587 views in the report, with 48 active users. The original question was how readers arrived and where they went next. GA4 showed 48 landing sessions attributed to google / organic, while Search Console recorded 16 text-based web search clicks. This report documents the investigation, the sitewide comparison, and what the available data support. Secorine Syrup page

How we checked

  1. Fix the date range and site scope. Both reports used November 12–18, 2026. The GA4 property uses Taiwan time (UTC+8). Search Console daily data use Pacific time, which was PST (UTC−8) in this period. The matching date labels therefore have boundaries 16 hours apart. The sitewide comparison covered /pteduimg/ on liaoweihung.github.io and excluded other tools on that host. Google's guide to reporting discrepancies
  2. Use GA4 to examine views, paths, and landing sessions. We read page views in Pages and screens and set the page title as a starting or ending point in Path exploration. In Free form, Landing page exactly matched /pteduimg/cards/ped_cold_secorine.html and Session source / medium exactly matched google / organic. For the sitewide comparison, the landing page matched ^/pteduimg/.*$ on the production hostname. A separate view grouped page referrers with Event name = session_start. GA4 path exploration · GA4 sessions and attribution
  3. Check clicks, search types, and the canonical URL in Search Console. The Search results performance report used an exact full-URL filter for the page and the https://liaoweihung.github.io/pteduimg/ prefix for the site. We checked text-based web, image, web multimodal, video, and news search separately, reviewed daily, device, and country breakdowns, and confirmed the Google-selected canonical in URL Inspection. Search Console performance report and search types
  4. Compare with other pages and spot-check two days. We subtracted the Secorine page's counts from the corresponding sitewide sessions and click totals. For the seven sessions on November 12 and 14, we examined geography, city, operating system, browser, engagement time, and the New / returning dimension. We cross-checked the classification with the First visits metric (first_visit). GA4 user metrics

What we tried and found

Check Finding and interpretation
Previous and next pages The leading previous-page nodes were 勝克敏液 with 9 events and Meptin Liquid with 8. The leading next-page nodes were Meptin Liquid with 14, 勝克敏液 with 8, and the homepage with 2. Path exploration reports event counts.
Images, Lens, video, and news Search Console recorded 2 image clicks and 2 web multimodal clicks, a category that includes Lens and Circle to Search. Video and news search each showed 0 clicks. The multimodal report provides no separate Lens count.
Discover, referrers, and attribution This property did not display a Discover report, so Discover traffic could not be quantified. Of the 48 organic-attributed session_start records, 46 had https://www.google.com/ as the referrer and 2 had the on-site Meptin Liquid page. Multiple Google surfaces can send the Google referrer. The internal referrers show that the recorded referrer and session attribution can differ, without establishing the session history.
Time zone and reporting updates Search Console showed 1 text-based web click for all of November 11 in Pacific time. Using the reported data, alignment to Taiwan dates could increase the original 16 clicks to at most 17. A reread still showed 6 clicks on November 17 and 4 on November 18. Later revisions were not checked.
Production hostname and canonical URL All 48 sessions were on the production hostname. Google selected the .html canonical. The extensionless URL also loaded and declared the .html URL canonical. A broader GSC filter for URLs containing ped_cold_secorine still showed one row and 16 text-based web clicks.
Geography, devices, and engagement Of 48 sessions, 41 were from Taiwan, 38 were on mobile devices, and 34 were engaged. In the seven-session spot check, six were from Taiwan and six were engaged, with 25 seconds of average engagement time. All seven were new in GA4, with seven first_visit events. The sample showed no large cluster of low-engagement overseas traffic and weakens the old-tab explanation for the same GA4 identity.
Search Console device and country breakdowns The page's text-based web total was 16. The device breakdown showed 8 clicks (5 mobile, 3 desktop, 0 tablet), and the country breakdown also showed 8 (5 Taiwan, 3 United States). Neither breakdown provides a complete distribution. Equal subtotals do not establish that the same clicks were omitted.

See Google's referrer documentation, the Discover report eligibility criteria, and Search Console guidance on differences between chart and table totals.

Conclusion

The difference between GA4 sessions and Search Console clicks was larger for the Secorine page, which also had a higher GA4 engagement rate. The page had 48 sessions and 20 clicks (2.40 times), compared with 24 and 17 (1.41 times) for the rest of the site. Engagement rates were 71% and 33%, respectively. The ratios compare report counts and have no universal benchmark. Engagement indicates that a session met GA4's criteria and cannot establish reader identity, intent, or educational benefit. GA4 engagement rate definition

The cause remains unresolved. These reports cannot match clicks and sessions one to one, quantify Discover traffic, rule out tracking issues, or establish that Search Console undercounted clicks. The seven first_visit events establish new visits as recognized by GA4. The referrer was not cross-checked for those exact seven visits, and the count does not establish seven distinct individuals. For routine monitoring, use Search Console for impressions, clicks, queries, and position, and GA4 for engagement and on-site browsing. The investigation used existing reports, and this public record presents aggregate findings. Google's guidance on using both reports · GA4 reporting identity
